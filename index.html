01

Context & problem

Previous copy on the booking card

“इस कनेक्शन पर ₹750 कमाएँ”

Meant to signal access to a higher-earning scheme — but it reads as a flat ₹750 payout for accepting this one connection.

  • ₹750 is a scheme-based opportunity, not a guaranteed payout for a single connection.
  • Higher earning lands in month 1; the same connection earns nothing extra in months 2 and 3.
  • The card never explained this, so the scheme page feels like it's walking back the promise.
  • Goal: keep the ₹750 hook, but make expectations accurate before the CSP opens details.
Design objective Communicate quickly that (1) there's an earning opportunity, (2) ₹750 is the upper limit, and (3) it's tied to the scheme's first-month benefit — without overloading the card.
02

Copy principle

Two words control expectation while preserving the hook.

तक

Makes ₹750 an upper potential, not an unconditional promise.

मौका

Frames it as an opportunity, not an automatic payout.

Subtitle

Adds timing — the benefit is concentrated in month 1.

A · B · C

Vary hierarchy: connection-, benefit-, amount-first.

03

Variants

AConnection-first
इस कनेक्शन से ₹750 तक कमाएँ
पहले महीने में ₹750 तक की कमाई

The connection is the primary context. Tells the CSP this booking carries an opportunity, with “तक” keeping ₹750 from reading as unconditional.

Best when the earning should feel directly relevant to the booking being evaluated.

BBenefit-first
ज़्यादा कमाई का मौका
पहले महीने में ₹750 तक की कमाई पाएँ

The broader benefit leads; ₹750 follows as the concrete first-month figure.

Best for expectation management — though the hook is less specific than A or C.

CAmount-first Best balance
₹750 तक कमाने का मौका
स्कीम के तहत पहले महीने में ज़्यादा कमाई पाएँ

₹750 is the strongest hook; “तक” + “मौका” stop it sounding guaranteed. The subtitle names the scheme so the CSP knows where ₹750 comes from.

Best balance between attention and expectation-setting.

05

What each variant tests

A

Does tying the opportunity directly to the connection improve relevance and engagement?

B

Does leading with the broader benefit reduce reading ₹750 as a direct connection payout?

C

Can the strongest numeric hook survive without the old expectation, using “तक”, “मौका” and an explicit scheme reference?

06

Intended understanding

All three should land the same message: this booking presents a scheme-based earning opportunity; ₹750 is the upper amount available in the first month, subject to the scheme's conditions.
